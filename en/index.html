About the Counselor

Counselor Gamal Abdel Nasser Sadek is a lawyer and legal advisor with more than seven years of experience, specializing in company formation and contract drafting, as well as civil, criminal, family and personal status cases.

He holds specialized diplomas in company formation and contract drafting. The office has formed more than 75 companies across sectors including real estate investment, contracting, jewelry, gold bullion, workforce transport and digital marketing.

He also publishes legal content on his YouTube channel, helping individuals and businesses understand the law and legal procedures through clear, simple explanations.

Company Formation

From idea to Commercial Registry, in clear steps

We choose with you the entity that fits your activity, number of partners and liability, then handle the whole process.

Entities we form

  • Single-Person Company

    One founder, with liability limited to the company's capital.

  • Limited Liability Company

    Two or more partners, each liable up to their share.

  • Joint Stock Company

    For larger ventures, with capital divided into shares.

  • Partnerships

    General and limited partnerships, built on trust between partners.

Formation stages

  1. 1

    First consultation

    We understand your plan and set out requirements and timeline.

  2. 2

    Choosing the entity

    We compare legal forms and recommend the best fit.

  3. 3

    Trade name

    We check the proposed name and follow its approval.

  4. 4

    Memorandum

    We draft the founding contract to protect every partner.

  5. 5

    Registration

    Commercial Registry entry and tax registration.

  6. 6

    After formation

    Contracts, amendments, investor residency and ongoing support.
