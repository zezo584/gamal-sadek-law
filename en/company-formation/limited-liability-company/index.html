Company Formation · The most common form for partnerships

Limited Liability Company (LLC)

The best fit for most small and medium partnerships: two to fifty partners, each liable up to their share of the capital, with flexible management and profit distribution.

Request a Consultation WhatsApp

At a glance

Partners
2 to 50 partners
Liability
Up to each partner's share
Management
One or more managers, partners or not
Shares
Not offered for public subscription

Suits

  • Two or more partners starting a joint business
  • Family businesses
  • Medium-sized trading and service businesses

Advantages

  • Protects partners' personal assets
  • Flexible management and profit-sharing through the memorandum
  • Simpler procedures than a joint stock company

Keep in mind

  • Shares cannot be offered to the public
  • Share transfers are restricted and partners have pre-emption rights

Questions about Limited Liability Company (LLC)

Requirements vary by entity and activity; we send the exact list for your case after the first consultation.

What are the key clauses of an LLC memorandum?

Share allocation, manager powers, profit and loss distribution, and rules for share transfers and partner exits. Drafting them precisely prevents most later disputes.

Can the manager be a non-partner?

Yes. A manager may be appointed from the partners or from outside, as the memorandum provides.

Can a new partner join after formation?

Yes, through a share transfer or capital increase and an amendment of the memorandum. We handle the whole process.
