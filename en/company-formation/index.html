Capital companies

Single-Person Company

A company owned by one natural or legal person, whose founder is liable for its obligations only up to the capital allocated to it. It lets you operate through an independent legal entity without partners.

Owners
One owner
Liability
Up to the company's capital
Management
The owner or an appointed manager
Trade name
Derived from its purpose or the owner's name

Suits: An entrepreneur starting alone · A sole trader who wants to separate personal assets from the business · An existing company wanting a wholly owned arm

Limited Liability Company (LLC)

The best fit for most small and medium partnerships: two to fifty partners, each liable up to their share of the capital, with flexible management and profit distribution.

Capital companies

Joint Stock Company

A company whose capital is divided into equal, tradable shares, with each shareholder liable only up to the value of their shares. It suits ventures needing larger capital or many investors.

Shareholders
At least three
Liability
Up to the value of shares
Management
Board of directors and general assembly
Capital
Divided into tradable shares

Suits: Large and investment-driven projects · Companies planning to bring in investors or expand · Major real estate and industrial projects

Partnerships

Partnerships (General & Limited)

Companies built on personal trust between partners. In a general partnership all partners are personally and jointly liable for the company's debts; a limited partnership has at least one general partner and a limited partner liable only up to their share.

Types
General and limited partnership
General partner liability
Personal, joint and unlimited
Limited partner liability

Firm name

Suits: Small family and professional partnerships · Partners with strong trust and prior acquaintance · A financing partner who wants no management role (limited partnership)

Company formation FAQ

How much does company formation cost?

The cost is made of government fees (notarization, Commercial Registry, publication, tax registration and activity-specific requirements) plus the office's fees, and varies by entity, capital and activity. We send a written quote for the total after the first consultation.

How long does company formation take?

It depends on the entity, the activity, document readiness and any approvals required. We set a clear timeline in the first consultation and follow it.

What is the difference between a single-person company and an LLC?

A single-person company has one founder whose liability is limited to the company's capital, while an LLC has two or more partners, each liable up to their share. We help you choose based on your plan and partners.

Can foreigners form a company in Egypt?

Yes, in most activities, with specific conditions for some activities and areas. We review your activity and the partners' nationalities before starting, and after formation we handle investor residency for foreign partners.

Do I need to be present in person?

Many procedures can be handled under an official power of attorney. We will tell you in the consultation which steps may need your presence.

What does the memorandum of association include?

Partners' details, name, activity, premises, capital and shares, management, profit and loss distribution, and rules for partners joining or leaving. Drafting these terms precisely is what protects the partnership later.

Do you offer services after formation?

Yes: employment, supply and partnership contracts, company data amendments and ongoing legal affairs.
