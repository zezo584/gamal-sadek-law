Company Formation · One founder, limited liability

Single-Person Company

A company owned by one natural or legal person, whose founder is liable for its obligations only up to the capital allocated to it. It lets you operate through an independent legal entity without partners.

Request a Consultation WhatsApp

At a glance

Owners
One owner
Liability
Up to the company's capital
Management
The owner or an appointed manager
Trade name
Derived from its purpose or the owner's name

Suits

  • An entrepreneur starting alone
  • A sole trader who wants to separate personal assets from the business
  • An existing company wanting a wholly owned arm

Advantages

  • Protects your personal assets from business debts
  • Fast decisions with no partner approvals
  • A formal entity that eases dealings with banks and clients

Keep in mind

  • Partners can only join by changing the legal form
  • Company and owner funds must be kept strictly separate

Questions about Single-Person Company

Requirements vary by entity and activity; we send the exact list for your case after the first consultation.

Can a single-person company later become an LLC?

Yes. The legal form can be changed when partners join, and we handle the conversion and contract amendments.

Can an existing company form a single-person company?

Yes. The owner may be a legal person, such as an existing company forming a wholly owned subsidiary.

What is the minimum capital?

Capital requirements are set by law and its regulations and may change. We confirm the current requirement for your activity in the first consultation.
